This project provides two integrated services:

### 1.1 RAG-Based Research Report Generation System
- Automated research report generation service, integrating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Large Language Models (LLM), web search capabilities, and structured content generation.
- Generates research reports based on web-sourced information and user-specified topics.

### 1.2 Live2D Interactive Chatbot
- Live2D chatbot service, integrating Large Language Models (LLM), Live2D animated characters, and Text-to-Speech (TTS).
